What speech to text really costs for NotebookLM captions

Captioning a NotebookLM overview is cheaper than most people assume, because the thing you send is not the video. This page explains what actually crosses the network, what the free tiers allow, and when a paid key becomes worth it.

SA Sarang (@sarang-cmd), creator of LM Subs. Published 2026-10-26. 486 words.

You send audio, not video

A ten minute Video Overview is roughly 50 MB as an MP4. LM Subs reads the audio track out of the stream in your browser and sends that instead, which for the same overview is around 3 MB. Smaller uploads mean faster responses, fewer rejected requests, and a much lower chance of hitting a provider's payload cap.

Free tiers that cover normal use

Provider Free plan Good to know
Groq Permanent free plan, no card Generous daily audio allowance, so most people never pay
Mistral Free Experiment plan No card, needs a phone verification step
Local Whisper Unlimited Runs on your machine, no provider involved at all

Free tier limits change without notice. Check the provider's own pricing page before you plan around a number, and treat the queue in LM Subs as your safety net rather than any single free plan.

When paying starts to make sense

  • You caption more than a handful of long Audio Overviews every day.
  • You need word level timestamps for a language the free models handle poorly.
  • You are captioning for a team and do not want to share one free key.
  • You want a larger model than the free tier exposes.

Paid speech to text is billed per minute of audio, and the per minute prices are low enough that a long overview costs a fraction of a cent on most providers. The real cost driver is volume, not the individual video.

How to keep the bill at zero

  1. Order the queue by costPut Groq first, Mistral second, and a local server last. Failover moves down the list only when a provider refuses the request.
  2. Store one key per providerTwo configured providers beat one, because a rate limit on the first no longer stops your work.
  3. Reuse the transcriptLM Subs caches generated captions per media item, so replaying an overview does not pay for it twice.
  4. Caption what you needAuto generation is a switch. Turn it off if you only need captions occasionally.

Frequently asked questions

Is LM Subs itself free?

Yes. The extension is free and MIT licensed. You pay only if you choose a provider that charges, and the recommended starting providers both have permanent free tiers.

What happens when a free key runs out of quota?

The provider returns a rate limit response, LM Subs marks that provider as unavailable for the request, and the next provider in your queue takes over automatically.

Does an Audio Overview cost more than a Video Overview?

Billing is per minute of audio, so a twenty minute podcast costs more than a three minute video simply because it is longer. There is no separate charge for the Audio Overview format.
